How it works

Name someone who shared a roster with the start player, then someone who shared a roster with them, until you reach the target.

Two players are teammates if they were on the same team's roster in the same season — regular season, practice squad, or a stint that ended in a cut. They did not have to play a snap together.

Downs. In the daily and on hard, a wrong guess costs a down. Convert a link and the chains move — four fresh downs. Burn all four on one link and it is a turnover. Easy and medium free play have no downs; guess freely.

Par is the fewest links possible — some days it is one link, most days two. Reach the target and it is a touchdown; do it at par with no wrong guesses and no hints and it is a perfect drive.

Rosters cover 2000–2026, so only stints from those seasons count.
