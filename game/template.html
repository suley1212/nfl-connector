How it works

You get a start player and a target player. Name someone who shared a roster with the start player, then someone who shared a roster with them, and keep going until you reach the target.

Two players count as teammates if they appear on the same team's roster in the same season — regular season, practice squad, or a stint that ended in a cut. They did not have to play a snap together.

Par is the fewest links the puzzle can be solved in. Beating par is not possible; matching it is the goal.

Rosters cover 2000–2026, so only teams and stints from those seasons count. Tom Brady connects through New England and Tampa Bay, not Michigan.
